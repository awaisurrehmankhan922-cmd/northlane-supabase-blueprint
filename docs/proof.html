Both locks

The server checks every rule, and then the database checks it again under the signed-in person’s own identity. A bug in one is caught by the other.

Who you are comes from the token

Branch, roles and powers are stamped into the sign-in token by the server. No rule ever trusts something the person could edit.

Nothing is lost, in the database too

No delete at all. Money, logs and the change trail refuse edits from everyone, administrator included. Mistakes are reversed; records are archived.

Who sees what Briefing pages 16 and 19

Branches fully separate; Founder, CEO and Director shared; the second sign-in step required for everything.

  1. ✔Someone who typed the right password but not the authenticator code sees nothing at all, on every one of the 17 tables, and cannot save anything.
  2. ✔A Bradford lettings manager asks for "everything": gets Bradford contacts only, plus the company-wide Help list. No Leeds record appears.
  3. ✔A Leeds lettings manager sees Leeds contacts only.
  4. ✔A Founder and the CEO see both branches.
  5. ✔A Bradford person sends a hand-made request to save a Leeds contact: refused. Tries to change a Leeds contact: nothing changes.
  6. ✔A basic staff member may add a contact (ticked for them) but cannot change one (not ticked), and cannot touch tenancies at all.
  7. ✔Nobody can delete a record, not even a Founder. The database does not have a delete permission for staff at all.
  8. ✔A Bradford person named on a Leeds viewing can see that one appointment; a Bradford colleague not named on it cannot.
  9. ✔The security log is invisible without the "Read the security log" power.
  10. ✔ID documents are invisible without the "View ID documents" power; ordinary documents need only the right branch.

The money rules Briefing page 8

Insert-only ledger, reversals, reference numbers, closed months, two accounts, whole pence.

  1. ✔Taking rent gives a receipt number like NL-R-0001. Someone without the "Take rent" power is refused.
  2. ✔Without "See Accounts" you see only the receipts you issued yourself; with it, the whole ledger.
  3. ✔A money line can never be changed or deleted: not by staff, not by a Founder, not even by the database administrator typing raw commands.
  4. ✔"Reverse" writes a mirror entry (NL-RV-…) in the same transaction; the two add up to zero; the reversal is in the security log.
  5. ✔You cannot reverse the same line twice, reverse a reversal, reverse without "See Accounts", or reverse without saying why.
  6. ✔A Bradford accounts person cannot reverse a Leeds entry; a Founder can.
  7. ✔25 people take a statement number at the same instant: 25 different numbers, in order, no gaps.
  8. ✔The second branch’s numbers carry its code: NL-B2-J-0001. A Bradford person cannot take a Leeds number.
  9. ✔A number stays used for good, even if the record it was on is erased.
  10. ✔Once September is closed, nobody can date money into it, except a Founder.
  11. ✔A money line of £0.00 is refused. An amount with a fraction of a penny is refused.
  12. ✔Rent sent to the business account: refused. A fee in the business account without a recorded move: refused.
  13. ✔£1,000 rent came in for a tenancy. Paying the landlord £1,000.01: refused ("only 100000p is held"). Fee out, rest to the landlord, then nothing more. Another tenancy’s money cannot be used.

Saving, caps, archive, privacy Briefing pages 7, 8, 10, 12, 15

Deposit caps, the change trail, archive and restore, masked bank details, erasure, live updates.

  1. ✔The deposit cap formula in the database and the one in the browser agree on 202 different rents, including a penny either side of the £50,000 line.
  2. ✔A tenancy saved with a deposit one penny over five weeks’ rent: refused by the database itself, whatever the screen allowed. Holding deposit over one week: refused. Weekly rent: refused.
  3. ✔Every save is kept with who did it, what it was before and after, and the version number goes up. Staff cannot read or edit the trail.
  4. ✔A deleted tenancy is in the Archive with all its data. Restoring it needs the "Restore from Archive" power. Someone without "Delete" on contacts cannot archive one. Leeds cannot archive Bradford’s.
  5. ✔A landlord’s bank details show as ****5678 / **-**-84 to most staff; in full to someone with "See bank details", and every full view is written to the security log. Leeds sees nothing.
  6. ✔A save batch is one transaction. Two people editing the same record from the same version: the second is refused and reloads. A batch with one bad change saves nothing at all.
  7. ✔Call events can only be written by the server. Staff can add notes to a call and end it, but cannot change who called.
  8. ✔Opening a passport copy is logged every time; opening a gas certificate is not.
  9. ✔An erasure request wipes the person’s record and redacts the trail (never deletes it), needs "Handle rights requests", is refused under a legal hold, and is recorded. Nothing else can touch the trail.
  10. ✔Every save is announced inside the database, so a second server can tell every open browser.

Email, texts and signing from a phone Briefing pages 10, 20, 23

Microsoft 365 and Postmark providers, bounces, text codes, the public signing route.

  1. ✔Microsoft 365: signs in, sends as the office mailbox with the PDF attached, tags each email with its outbox line.
  2. ✔If Microsoft refuses, staff see a plain message; after three tries the email goes to "Emails to send by hand".
  3. ✔The alternative service (Postmark) sends, and its bounce report marks the right outbox line as bounced with the reason.
  4. ✔A bounce report without the secret key is refused.
  5. ✔Microsoft’s "undeliverable" replies are matched back to the right outbox line.
  6. ✔In test mode the email is written to a file and nothing leaves the CRM.
  7. ✔Text codes go to UK mobiles only; a landline or a mistyped number is refused.
  8. ✔A tenant signing from their phone sees only their own pages and boxes, never the guarantor’s, never the office note.
  9. ✔"Not your turn yet" until the person before has signed. Five wrong codes lock it; staff issue a fresh code.
  10. ✔The server saves only that signer’s boxes, refuses someone else’s, insists on the required ones, and stamps the time and address itself.
  11. ✔More than 20 tries a minute from one connection is blocked.

The desk phones Briefing page 21

Asterisk/TINKLE events → the CRM’s own phone endpoint, verified and de-duplicated.

  1. ✔Caller numbers in any format become +44…; a withheld number is kept as "withheld".
  2. ✔A call rings 201, is answered on 202, is transferred to 203: the CRM gets ringing, answered, transferred. Calls between desks are ignored.
  3. ✔A caller hanging up before anyone answers is a missed call.
  4. ✔End to end: a fake phone system sends a call; the bridge posts exactly what the CRM’s phone endpoint expects, with the secret key header.
  5. ✔If the supplier pushes events instead: no key, refused; an old replayed event, ignored; a good one reaches the CRM.
  6. ✔A forged event with a bad signature is refused.
  7. ✔If the supplier offers nothing, the handsets themselves can report incoming / answered / ended; still key-protected.
  8. ✔The phone system often sends the same event twice; the CRM is told once.
